How KrishiSetu works

1

List directly

Farmers & FPOs list harvest with grade, quantity and an AI-suggested fair price — no commission agents, no distress sale.

2

Order direct

Consumers order retail baskets; restaurants, messes & retailers buy wholesale with bulk quotes straight from the farm.

3

Optimised delivery

Orders aggregate at collection hubs; AI forecasts demand per crop and the router sequencing engine (NN + 2-opt) plans each vehicle's route.
